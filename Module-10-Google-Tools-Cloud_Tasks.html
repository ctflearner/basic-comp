Task 1: Google Drive — Organize and Share Files

Instructions

  1. Open Google Drive (drive.google.com)
  2. Upload 5 files from your computer to Drive. These can be:
  3. A photo
  4. A PDF (any document you have)
  5. A Word document (from Module 7)
  6. An Excel file (from Module 8)
  7. A PowerPoint file (from Module 9)
  8. Create 3 folders in Drive:
  9. "Course Documents"
  10. "Photos"
  11. "Shared Files"
  12. Move the files into the appropriate folders:
  13. Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files into "Course Documents"
  14. Photo into "Photos"
  15. PDF into "Shared Files"
  16. Share the "Shared Files" folder with a friend or classmate:
  17. Give them Viewer access
  18. Send them the invitation via email

Expected Result

  • 5 files uploaded to Google Drive
  • 3 folders created and organized
  • 1 folder shared with another person
  • You can see the shared folder in your Drive with a "shared" icon

Bonus Challenge

  • Download the Google Drive desktop app (drive.google.com/download)
  • Set up Google Drive on your phone (install Google Drive app from Play Store)
  • Take a photo on your phone and verify it appears in Drive on your computer

Task 2: Google Docs — Write, Format, and Collaborate

Instructions

  1. Open Google Docs (docs.google.com)
  2. Create a new blank document
  3. Name it: "My First Google Doc"
  4. Write a 200-word essay on one of these topics:
  5. "Why I Am Learning Digital Skills"
  6. "How Technology Changed My Life"
  7. "My Dream Career"
  8. Format the document:
  9. Add a title using Heading 1 style
  10. Use bold for at least 3 important words
  11. Create a bullet list with at least 4 items
  12. Change the font to Arial, size 12
  13. Add a horizontal line after the title (Insert > Horizontal line)
  14. Share the document:
  15. Click Share > change "General access" to "Anyone with the link"
  16. Set permission to Commenter
  17. Copy the link and send it to a friend or classmate
  18. Ask your friend to add a comment on any sentence in the document
  19. Reply to their comment from your side

Expected Result

  • A formatted 200-word document in Google Docs
  • Document shared as "Anyone with link — Commenter"
  • At least 1 comment from another person
  • Your reply to that comment

Bonus Challenge

  • Use Suggesting mode (click the pencil icon > "Suggesting") to make a change to your own document, then accept the suggestion
  • Check Version history (File > Version history) and observe that Google tracked your edits

Task 3: Google Sheets — Monthly Budget with Formulas and Chart

Instructions

  1. Open Google Sheets (sheets.google.com)
  2. Create a new spreadsheet named: "My Monthly Budget"
  3. Set up the following structure:
A B
Category Amount (Rs.)
Rent/Housing 8000
Food/Groceries 5000
Transport 2000
Phone/Internet 1000
Clothing 1500
Entertainment 1000
Education 2000
Savings 3000
Miscellaneous 1500
Total (formula)
  1. Use formulas:
  2. In the Total cell, use =SUM(B2:B10) to calculate total expenses
  3. In a new row, type "Monthly Income" and enter 30000
  4. In the next row, use a formula to calculate "Remaining": =B12-B11 (Income minus Total)
  5. In another cell, calculate the savings percentage: =B9/B12*100
  6. Create a Pie Chart:
  7. Select cells A1:B10
  8. Insert > Chart > choose Pie chart
  9. Add a chart title: "Monthly Budget Breakdown"
  10. Format the sheet:
  11. Bold the header row
  12. Format the Amount column as currency (Format > Number > Currency)
  13. Add a background color to the header row
  14. Share the sheet:
  15. Share as "Anyone with the link — Viewer"

Expected Result

  • A spreadsheet with monthly budget data
  • Working SUM formula for total
  • Working formula for remaining balance
  • Working formula for savings percentage
  • A pie chart showing budget breakdown
  • Sheet shared as View Only

Bonus Challenge

  • Add conditional formatting: if the Remaining balance is negative, turn that cell red
  • Add a second sheet (tab at bottom) called "Yearly Summary" and calculate =B11*12 for annual expenses

Task 4: Google Forms — Event Feedback Survey

Instructions

  1. Open Google Forms (forms.google.com)
  2. Create a new blank form
  3. Title: "Event Feedback Survey"
  4. Description: "Thank you for attending! Please share your feedback to help us improve future events."
  5. Create 8 questions using different question types:
# Question Type
1 What is your name? Short answer
2 Email address Short answer (with email validation)
3 Which event did you attend? Multiple choice (give 4 options)
4 How would you rate the event overall? Linear scale (1 to 5)
5 What did you like most about the event? Paragraph
6 Which aspects need improvement? Checkboxes (give 5 options: Venue, Food, Speakers, Duration, Organization)
7 Would you attend again next year? Multiple choice (Yes, No, Maybe)
8 Any additional suggestions? Paragraph (not required)
  1. Make questions 1-7 Required
  2. Customize the form: Change the color theme using the palette icon
  3. Click "Send" and copy the form link
  4. Fill out the form yourself (open the link in an incognito/private window)
  5. Go back to the form editor and click "Responses" tab to see your submission
  6. Click the green Sheets icon to view responses in Google Sheets

Expected Result

  • A working Google Form with 8 questions of mixed types
  • Form link generated and shareable
  • At least 1 response submitted (your own)
  • Responses visible in the Responses tab and in Google Sheets

Bonus Challenge

  • Share the form link with 5 friends/classmates and collect their responses
  • In the Google Sheet with responses, create a chart showing the rating distribution
  • Turn the form into a Quiz (Settings gear > Quizzes > "Make this a quiz") and assign point values

Task 5: Real-Life Project — Create a Blog on Blogger

Instructions

This is your major project for Module 10. You will create a real blog that you can share with anyone and add to your resume.

Step 1: Set Up Your Blog (15 min)
1. Go to blogger.com and sign in
2. Click "Create New Blog"
3. Choose a topic you are passionate about. Ideas:
- Your learning journey (what you learn in this course)
- Tech tips for beginners
- Book or movie reviews
- Cooking recipes
- Travel experiences
- Career advice for students
4. Choose a blog title (make it catchy but clear)
5. Choose a URL (yourname-topic.blogspot.com)
6. Select a theme/template

Step 2: Customize Your Blog (15 min)
1. Go to Theme > Customize
2. Change the background color or image
3. Adjust fonts if needed
4. Add a short "About Me" description in the sidebar (Layout > Add a Gadget > About Me)

Step 3: Write 3 Blog Posts (45 min — 15 min each)

Each post MUST have:
- A clear, interesting title
- At least 200 words of content
- At least 1 image (use free images from unsplash.com or pexels.com)
- Proper formatting: headings, paragraphs, bold text where appropriate
- A closing line that invites readers to comment

Post suggestions based on your chosen topic:
- Post 1: Introduction — what the blog is about, why you started it
- Post 2: A helpful article related to your topic
- Post 3: A personal experience or opinion piece

Step 4: Review and Share (15 min)
1. Preview each post before publishing (Preview button)
2. Check that images load correctly
3. Check that text is readable (not too small, good contrast)
4. Publish all 3 posts
5. Copy your blog URL
6. Share it with at least 3 friends or family members via WhatsApp or email

Expected Result

  • A live blog on Blogger with a custom theme
  • 3 published blog posts (200+ words each, with images)
  • Blog URL shared with friends/family
  • At least 1 friend confirms they can see and read your blog

Bonus Challenge

  • Add a page (not post) called "About Me" with your bio (Pages > New page)
  • Add a link list widget to your sidebar with links to your social media profiles
  • Write a 4th post about something you learned in this course
