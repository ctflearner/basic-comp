Task 1: Setup + First Conversations with ChatGPT

Instructions

Step 1: Create Your Account (5 min)
1. Open Chrome and go to chat.openai.com
2. Click "Sign up"
3. Sign up using your Google account (click "Continue with Google")
4. Complete any verification steps
5. You are now on the ChatGPT interface — you should see a text box at the bottom where you can type

Step 2: Your First 3 Conversations (25 min)

Choose 3 topics that you genuinely find difficult in school, college, or any area of learning. For EACH topic, have a conversation with ChatGPT following these steps:

Conversation 1: A concept you find difficult
- Type: "Explain [topic] like I'm 15 years old. Use a real-life analogy and then give me the technical explanation."
- After it responds, ask: "Give me 3 examples to make this clearer"
- Then ask: "What are the most common mistakes people make when learning this?"

Conversation 2: A topic you want to learn more about
- Type: "I want to understand [topic] from scratch. Give me a 10-minute crash course covering the basics."
- Follow up: "Now give me 5 quiz questions to test myself"
- Follow up: "I got question 3 wrong. Explain why [your wrong answer] is wrong and why [right answer] is correct."

Conversation 3: Something practical
- Type: "Act as a [relevant expert]. Help me with [practical problem]."
- Follow up with 2-3 more questions to get deeper into the topic

Step 3: Rate the Responses

For each conversation, write down:

# Topic Was it helpful? (1-10) Was it accurate? (check with Google) What could you ask differently?

2
3

Expected Result

  • ChatGPT account created and working
  • 3 conversations completed on different topics
  • Rating table filled out for all 3 conversations
  • At least 1 insight about how to ask better questions

Bonus Challenge

  • Try asking the same question in 3 different ways and compare the responses. Which phrasing gave the best answer?
  • Ask ChatGPT: "What are 5 tips for writing better prompts for AI assistants?" — see if its advice matches what you learned in class

Task 2: Prompt Engineering Practice — From Vague to Specific

Instructions

This task teaches you the most important skill for using AI: writing good prompts. You will see how dramatically different the results are when you go from vague to specific.

Write 5 pairs of prompts: one VAGUE, one SPECIFIC. Send both to ChatGPT and compare.

Pair 1: Writing

Vague prompt (type this first):

Write about dogs.

Specific prompt (start a new chat, type this):

Write a 200-word informative paragraph about Golden Retrievers as family pets.
Cover: temperament, exercise needs, grooming requirements, and suitability
for families with young children. Tone: friendly and informative.
Target audience: someone considering their first pet.

Pair 2: Study Help

Vague prompt:

Help me study for my exam.

Specific prompt:

I have a Computer Networks exam in 3 days. The topics are:
OSI Model, TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, and Subnetting.
I'm weakest in Subnetting.

Create a 3-day study plan with:
- Day-by-day breakdown
- Key concepts to memorize for each topic
- 5 practice problems for Subnetting
- Memory tricks or mnemonics for the OSI layers
Available study time: 4 hours per day.

Pair 3: Email

Vague prompt:

Write an email to my boss.

Specific prompt:

Write a professional email to my team lead, Mr. Sharma, requesting to
work from home next Friday because I have a medical appointment at 2 PM.

Context: We usually work from office. I'll be available online for the
full day except during the appointment (2-3 PM). My current tasks
don't require in-person presence.

Tone: Polite and professional. Under 100 words.

Pair 4: Career Advice

Vague prompt:

What job should I get?

Specific prompt:

Act as a career counselor for Indian engineering students.

I'm a final-year BTech CSE student with:
- Skills: Python, SQL, basic web development, Excel
- CGPA: 7.5
- No internship experience
- Interests: data analysis and cybersecurity
- Location preference: Bangalore or Hyderabad

Give me:
1. Top 5 entry-level roles I should apply for
2. Expected salary range for each
3. Top 3 companies hiring for these roles
4. Skills I should add in the next 3 months to improve my chances
5. A 3-month action plan to become job-ready

Pair 5: Daily Life

Vague prompt:

Help me save money.

Specific prompt:

Help me create a savings plan.

My situation:
- Monthly salary: Rs. 22,000
- Rent: Rs. 7,000 (shared room)
- Food: Rs. 4,000 (I cook at home mostly)
- Transport: Rs. 1,500 (bus/metro)
- Phone plan: Rs. 500
- I want to save for a laptop (Rs. 50,000) within 8 months

Give me:
1. A monthly budget in table format
2. Areas where I can cut costs
3. A savings tracker format
4. How much I need to save monthly to reach my laptop goal
5. 5 practical tips to reduce daily spending

  • Clear comparison showing how specific prompts produce dramatically better results
  • Completed comparison table
  • Understanding of what makes a good prompt (specificity, context, format, role, constraints)

Bonus Challenge

  • Create your own 6th pair on any topic of your choice
  • Write the "perfect prompt" for getting ChatGPT to help you with your weakest academic subject
  • Try using the role assignment technique: compare "Explain quantum computing" vs "Act as a physics teacher for 10th graders. Explain quantum computing using analogies a teenager would understand."

Task 3: Job Application Package — Resume, Cover Letter, Interview Prep

Instructions

Use ChatGPT to create a complete job application package for YOUR real background. This is not a hypothetical exercise — create materials you can actually use.

Step 1: Choose a Real Job (10 min)

Go to naukri.com or linkedin.com/jobs and find a real job posting that:
- Matches your field of study or interest
- Is an entry-level or fresher role
- Is in a city you would consider working in

Copy the job title, company name, and key requirements.

Step 2: Create Your Resume (20 min)

Send this prompt to ChatGPT (fill in YOUR real details):

Create a professional, ATS-friendly resume for me.

My details:
- Name: [Your real name]
- Education: [Your real degree, university, year, CGPA/percentage]
- Skills: [List your actual skills — include everything from this course:
  Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Google tools, Python basics, HTML, basic cybersecurity awareness]
- Projects: [List any projects — include the ones from this course:
  Monthly budget spreadsheet, Personal portfolio website,
  Blog on Blogger, Python programs]
- Certifications: [Any online courses completed]
- Languages: [Languages you speak]
- Location: [Your city]

Target role: [Paste the job title you found]
Requirements from the job posting: [Paste key requirements]

Format: Single page, clean professional layout, clear sections.
Use strong action verbs. Quantify achievements where possible.

Refine the resume:
- "Make the summary section more impactful"
- "Add the course projects with better descriptions — make them sound professional"
- "Adjust skills to match the job requirements better"

Step 3: Create Your Cover Letter (15 min)

Write a cover letter for the [job title] position at [company name].

Use the resume you just created as my background.
The job requires: [paste 3-4 key requirements from the posting]

Tone: Professional but showing genuine enthusiasm.
Length: Under 250 words.
Show why I'm a good fit even as a fresher — emphasize my willingness
to learn and the practical projects I've completed.

Step 4: Interview Preparation (15 min)

I have an interview for [job title] at [company name].

Based on my resume and the job requirements, give me:

1. 10 most likely technical interview questions with sample answers
   (focus on [your top 2-3 skills])
2. 5 behavioral/HR questions with sample answers using the STAR method
   (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
3. 3 smart questions I should ask the interviewer
4. Tips specific to interviews at [company name] or similar companies
5. Common mistakes freshers make and how to avoid them

Expected Result

  • A complete, professional resume tailored to a real job posting
  • A cover letter specific to that role and company
  • 10 technical + 5 behavioral interview questions with answers
  • 3 questions to ask the interviewer
  • All materials saved (copy from ChatGPT and paste into a Google Doc or Word document)

Bonus Challenge

  • Ask ChatGPT for a mock interview: "Let's practice. Ask me one question at a time and rate my answers."
  • Create a second resume version for a different type of role (e.g., one for data analyst, one for web developer)
  • Ask: "Review my resume and tell me what's missing or weak" — paste your resume back and get feedback

Task 4: Daily Productivity — 5 Real-Life Tasks in One Day

Instructions

Use ChatGPT for 5 real, practical tasks TODAY. These must be tasks you actually need done, not made-up exercises.

Task 4.1: Draft an Email (any email you actually need to send)

Examples:
- Email to a professor about a deadline extension
- Email to a company about an internship inquiry
- Email to a friend organizing a group outing
- Email to customer support about a product issue

Prompt template:

Write a [tone] email to [person/role] about [topic].
Context: [brief situation].
Length: Under [number] words.

Screenshot the prompt and response.

Task 4.2: Plan a Meal

Ask ChatGPT to help with your actual dinner or tomorrow's meals:

I want to cook dinner tonight. I have: [list actual ingredients in your kitchen].
Give me 2 recipes I can make in under 30 minutes.
I prefer [your cuisine preference].
Give me step-by-step instructions.

Screenshot the prompt and response. Bonus: actually cook one of the recipes.

Task 4.3: Explain a Concept

Choose something you genuinely need to understand for school, work, or personal interest:

Explain [concept] in simple terms with 3 real-world examples.
I'm a [your level] learner.
Then give me a quick quiz (3 questions) to test my understanding.

Screenshot the prompt and response.

Task 4.4: Create a To-Do List or Plan

Ask ChatGPT to help organize something real in your life:

Help me organize my tasks for this week.
Here's everything I need to do: [list your actual tasks]
Prioritize them by urgency and importance.
Create a day-by-day plan.
Estimate time needed for each.

Screenshot the prompt and response.

Task 4.5: Get Advice on a Real Decision

Ask ChatGPT about a decision you are actually facing:

I need advice on [real decision]. Here's my situation: [explain].
Give me:
- Pros and cons
- Things I might not have considered
- Your recommendation and why

Screenshot the prompt and response.

Expected Result

  • 5 real ChatGPT conversations completed for actual daily needs
  • Screenshots of each conversation (prompt + response)
  • A brief note for each: "How useful was this? What would I change about my prompt?"
  • At least 3 of the 5 outputs were genuinely useful in your real life

Bonus Challenge

  • Use ChatGPT for 2 more tasks you think of during the day
  • After completing all tasks, ask ChatGPT: "Based on our conversations today, what patterns do you see in my interests and needs? What AI tools or skills should I learn next?"

Task 5: Fact-Check Exercise — Can You Trust AI?

Instructions

This task teaches you the most critical skill: knowing when AI is wrong.

Step 1: Ask ChatGPT 10 Factual Questions (20 min)

Ask these questions one at a time in ChatGPT. Write down its answers.

  1. "What is the population of India as of 2025?"
  2. "Who is the current Chief Minister of [your state]?"
  3. "What is the tallest building in Mumbai?"
  4. "When was the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay founded?"
  5. "What is the distance from Delhi to Mumbai by road?"
  6. "What is the current price of 1 gram of gold in India?"
  7. "How many Fundamental Rights are listed in the Indian Constitution?"
  8. "What programming language was created by Guido van Rossum?"
  9. "Name 3 rivers that flow through Bangalore."
  10. "What is the minimum age to vote in India?"

Step 2: Verify Each Answer with Google (15 min)

For each answer, Google the question and find the correct answer from a reliable source (government websites, Wikipedia, official sources).

Bonus Challenge

  • Ask: "Are you sure about that?" after any answer — see if it changes its response (sometimes it will flip even when it was right the first time!)

Task 6: Real-Life Project — "My AI-Powered Week"

Instructions

This is the final project of the entire course. For 7 consecutive days, use ChatGPT (or any AI tool) for at least one productive task each day. Keep a daily log and write a final reflection.

Daily Log Template

Create a Google Doc or Word document with this format:

# My AI-Powered Week — Daily Log

## Day 1: [Date]
**Task**: [What you needed to do]
**Prompt I Used**: [Exact prompt you typed]
**ChatGPT's Response** (summary): [Brief summary of what it gave you]
**Usefulness Rating**: [1-10, where 10 = exactly what I needed]
**What I'd Change About My Prompt**: [How you could have asked better]
**Did I Verify the Info?**: [Yes/No — if yes, was it accurate?]

## Day 2: [Date]
[same format]

... continue for all 7 days ...

Suggested Tasks for Each Day

You can use these or choose your own:

Day Suggested Task Category Example
Day 1 Learning Ask ChatGPT to explain a difficult topic from school/college
Day 2 Writing Draft an important email, message, or document
Day 3 Planning Create a study plan, travel plan, or weekly schedule
Day 4 Job/Career Work on resume, cover letter, or interview prep
Day 5 Daily Life Meal plan, budget, workout plan, or organize tasks
Day 6 Problem Solving Ask for advice on a real challenge or decision
Day 7 Creative Get help with a presentation, blog post, or creative project

Requirements

  • Use ChatGPT at least once per day for 7 days (you can use it more)
  • Each day's task must be DIFFERENT (do not repeat the same type of task)
  • Log must be filled in DAILY (do not fill it all at the end — the point is daily habit)
  • At least 3 of the 7 tasks must be for something you actually needed (not made up)
  • At least 2 prompts must use the "role assignment" technique
  • At least 1 task must include fact-checking the response

Final Reflection (Day 7 or 8)

After completing the 7-day log, write a 300-word reflection answering these questions:

  1. What was the most useful thing ChatGPT helped you with this week?
  2. What was the least useful or most inaccurate response you got?
  3. How did your prompting improve from Day 1 to Day 7? Give a specific example.
  4. In what areas of your life will you continue using AI tools?
  5. What limitations did you discover that you weren't aware of before?
  6. If you could teach one person in your family to use ChatGPT, what would be the first thing you'd show them?
  7. How do you think AI will affect your future career?

Expected Result

  • A complete 7-day log with daily entries (at least 7 entries)
  • Each entry includes: task, exact prompt, summary of response, rating, and prompt improvement note
  • A 300-word reflection covering all 7 questions
  • Evidence of improving prompt quality over the 7 days
  • At least 1 fact-checking entry

Bonus Challenge

  • Continue the log for 14 days instead of 7
  • Try Google Gemini or Perplexity AI for at least 2 of the days and compare with ChatGPT
  • Create a "Prompt Library" — a document with your 10 best prompts that you will reuse in the future, organized by category (study, work, personal)
  • Teach a friend or family member to use ChatGPT — document what you taught them and their reaction

Module Assessment

  3. What are the 5 rules of good prompting? Explain each briefly.
  4. Why is the "role assignment" technique effective? Give an example.
  5. What personal information should you NEVER share with ChatGPT?
